Getting your Timeline.json

Since late 2024 Google keeps your Timeline on the phone only — it is no longer in Takeout and there is no web download. The export button lives in the Maps app itself.

Android

  1. Open Settings → Location
  2. Tap Location services → Timeline
  3. Tap Export Timeline data
  4. Save it — it lands in Downloads as Timeline.json
  5. Move it to this computer over USB, Drive or Nearby Share

iPhone

  1. Open Google Maps, tap your profile picture
  2. Tap Your Timeline
  3. Tap the ⋯ menu, top right
  4. Tap Location & privacy settings → Export Timeline data
  5. Share it to this computer with AirDrop

Your data is never uploaded. The Timeline file is read inside your browser and never leaves this machine. The only requests this page makes are for the map imagery and an anonymous page counter — no cookies, no identifiers, and nothing whatsoever about your locations.

Reading this on the phone itself? Skip the transfer entirely — export, then tap Load and pick Timeline.json out of your files.

Android and iPhone write slightly different JSON. Both are handled; you do not need to convert anything.

Place names come from GeoNames (CC BY 4.0), matched on your own device against a list that ships with the page — your coordinates are never sent to a geocoder. Close-up imagery © Esri.

Not seeing the button? It only appears once Timeline has recorded something, and needs a recent Google Maps.
